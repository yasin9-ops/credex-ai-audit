# Prompts

## AI Summary Prompt

Generate a short AI spend audit summary.

Tool: {tool}
Plan: {plan}
Seats: {seats}
Savings: {savings}

Keep it professional and under 100 words.

## Why This Prompt

The prompt is intentionally short to reduce hallucinations and keep the output focused on savings insights.

## What Did Not Work

Longer prompts often generated repetitive financial advice and exceeded the desired summary length.
