# User Interviews

## Interview 1

**Name:** A.K.  
**Role:** Student Developer  
**Company Stage:** Personal Projects / Freelance

### Direct Quotes
- "I mainly use ChatGPT Plus and GitHub Copilot."
- "I never calculate how much I spend monthly on AI tools."
- "Sometimes I subscribe to tools and stop using them after a few weeks."

### Most Surprising Insight
The user was paying for multiple overlapping AI tools without comparing usage value or actual productivity gains.

### What It Changed About My Design
I simplified the audit form and focused on making the savings section visually prominent so users could immediately understand where money is being wasted.

## Interview 2

**Name:** R.S.  
**Role:** Final-Year Engineering Student  
**Company Stage:** Small Team Startup Internship

### Direct Quotes
- "Our team uses ChatGPT and Claude together for coding and documentation."
- "Nobody in our team tracks AI spending centrally."
- "A free audit tool would be useful before renewing subscriptions."

### Most Surprising Insight
The team had no shared visibility into AI subscription overlap, causing duplicate spending across team members.

### What It Changed About My Design
I added the team size and use-case fields to improve recommendation quality and make the audit more personalized.

## Interview 3

**Name:** M.P.  
**Role:** Indie Hacker / Side Project Builder  
**Company Stage:** Solo Founder

### Direct Quotes
- "AI tools save me time, but monthly subscriptions add up quickly."
- "I would definitely share a cost-saving report if it looked professional."
- "Most people don't realize how many subscriptions they actually pay for."

### Most Surprising Insight
The user cared more about clear visual savings reports than detailed technical analytics.

### What It Changed About My Design
I prioritized a clean dashboard UI with large monthly and annual savings numbers that are easy to screenshot and share.
