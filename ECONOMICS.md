# Economics

If an average converted customer generates $1000 in AI credit purchases annually, even a 5% conversion rate from audits to consultations could produce meaningful revenue.

Example:
- 1000 audits/month
- 10% consultation conversion
- 5% purchase conversion
- 50 paying customers annually

Estimated CAC:
- Organic content: low CAC
- Community outreach: moderate time investment
- Viral sharing via public audit URLs: scalable acquisition

To achieve $1M ARR, the product would require:
- Large-scale audit usage
- High-quality referral loops
- Strong startup founder adoption
