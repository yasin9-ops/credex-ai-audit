# Go-To-Market Strategy

The target users are early-stage startup founders and engineering managers with teams using multiple AI subscriptions.

These users typically search for:
- AI cost optimization
- Best AI coding tools
- Reduce SaaS spend
- ChatGPT alternatives

Potential acquisition channels:
- Indie Hackers
- Hacker News
- Reddit startup communities
- Twitter/X founder communities
- LinkedIn founder groups

To acquire the first 100 users:
1. Share screenshots of audit reports.
2. Post cost-saving examples publicly.
3. Reach out to startup communities.
4. Publish short comparison articles.

Week-1 traction success:
- 100 completed audits
- 20 shared reports
- 10 qualified leads
